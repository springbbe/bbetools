Online Stopwatch

A stopwatch accurate to 1/100 second, with laps.

The stopwatch runs right in your browser; its state is only saved on this device, never sent anywhere.

How to use

  1. Click Start or press Space.
  2. Click Lap (L key) to record lap times; the fastest lap is green, the slowest red.
  3. Stop, then click Reset (R key); download the lap table as CSV if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep running if I close the tab or turn off the screen?

Yes. Time follows the system clock, so reopening the page shows the right time; the state is saved in your browser.

How accurate is it?

It displays to 1/100 second. The main error comes from when you press the button (human reaction time is about 0.1–0.2 seconds).
