Watermark PDF

Stamp semi-transparent text or a logo onto PDF pages.

Your files are processed right on this device and never uploaded to any server.

Accepts: PDF

How to use

  1. Choose or drag and drop a PDF file.
  2. Type text or choose a logo; adjust opacity, angle, position, or tile it across the page.
  3. Preview it on the pages, click “Add watermark” and download the file.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every step runs in JavaScript right in your browser; files never leave your device.

Can the watermark be removed?

The watermark is drawn into the page content. Choose “Tile across the page” to make it harder to crop out.

Can I use a logo with a transparent background?

Yes. Use a PNG with a transparent background for the best look on any page background.

Can I watermark only some pages?

Yes. Choose “Some pages” and enter, e.g., 1-3, 5.
