Case Converter

Convert to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case, with full Unicode support.

The text you enter is only processed in your browser and never sent anywhere.

How to use

  1. Type or paste text into the box.
  2. Choose a case: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Capitalize Each Word, Sentence case or programming styles (camelCase, snake_case…).
  3. Copy or download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle accented letters correctly?

Yes. The tool uses Unicode case rules, so “crème brûlée” becomes “CRÈME BRÛLÉE” and back.

Do camelCase and snake_case keep accents?

No. Programming styles strip accents and special characters, e.g. “Phone number” becomes phoneNumber or phone_number.
