Countdown Timer

A countdown timer with an alarm and full-screen mode.

The timer runs right in your browser; settings are only saved on this device, never sent anywhere.

How to use

  1. Quick-pick 1, 3, 5… minutes or enter hours, minutes and seconds.
  2. Click Start (Space key); you can add 1 minute while it runs.
  3. When time's up the alarm rings until you click Mute alarm.

Frequently asked questions

Will I hear the alarm on another tab?

Yes, unless your device is muted. Click “Enable notifications when on another tab” to also get a system notification.

What if my phone's screen locks?

Mobile browsers usually pause pages when the screen locks, so the alarm may ring late. While counting, the page keeps the screen on (if the browser supports it).
