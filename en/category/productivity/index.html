Productivity & Random tools

Pomodoro, stopwatch, timer, spin the wheel, team picker, quick notes. 17 free tools that run right in your browser.

  • Pomodoro Timer

    A 25-minute focus, 5-minute break timer using the Pomodoro technique, with sounds, notifications and daily stats.

  • Online Stopwatch

    A stopwatch accurate to 1/100 second, with laps.

  • Countdown Timer

    A countdown timer with an alarm and full-screen mode.

  • Spin the Wheel

    Enter a list and spin a random wheel to pick a person, a meal or a prize winner.

  • Random Team Generator

    Split a list into random teams or draw a random order.

  • Coin Flip & Dice Roller

    Flip a coin or roll one or more random dice.

  • Random Number Generator

    Generate random numbers in a range, without duplicates, or run a raffle draw.

  • Quick Notes

    Notes saved right in your browser, auto-saved, downloadable as .txt.

  • Checklist

    A simple to-do list with drag-and-drop ordering, saved on your device.

  • World Clock & Time Zone Converter

    Current time in cities around the world, and time conversion between time zones.

  • Camera, Mic, Keyboard & Screen Test

    Test your camera, microphone, left/right speakers, keyboard, mouse and dead pixels on one page before an online meeting.

  • Webcam Test

    Test your webcam or laptop camera online: see the image, resolution, frame rate and take a test photo.

  • Microphone Test

    Test your microphone online: see the level and waveform as you speak, record 5 seconds and play it back.

  • Speaker & Headphone Test

    Test left and right speakers and headphones; play test tones at many frequencies and a low-to-high sweep.

  • Keyboard Test

    Test your keyboard online: Windows and Mac layouts, laptop or external, with or without a numpad; multi-key rollover and key chatter detection.

  • Mouse Test

    Test your mouse online: buttons (including Back/Forward), double-clicks, phantom double-click detection, wheel and polling rate.

  • Dead Pixel Test

    Find dead pixels, stuck pixels, backlight bleed and uneven colors with full-screen colors; see resolution and refresh rate.
