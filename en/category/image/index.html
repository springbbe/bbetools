Image tools

Compress, resize, crop, convert, HEIC to JPG, ID photos, background removal. 22 free tools that run right in your browser.

  • Compress Images

    Reduce the size of JPG, PNG and WebP images in bulk, with a mode to get below the KB you need.

  • Resize Image

    Shrink or enlarge images by pixels, percentage or longest side.

  • Crop Image

    Crop images freely or to 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:4 ratios.

  • Rotate & Flip Image

    Rotate images 90°, 180° or mirror-flip them in bulk.

  • Image Converter

    Convert between JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF.

  • HEIC to JPG

    Convert iPhone HEIC/HEIF photos to JPG or PNG in bulk, right on your device.

  • ID Photo Maker

    Make passport and ID photos from a portrait: AI background replacement, framing and a print sheet with multiple photos.

  • Remove Background

    Remove the background from portraits with AI running on your device; export transparent PNG or a colored background.

  • Blur & Redact Image

    Blur or black out ID numbers, license plates and faces before sending a photo.

  • Remove EXIF & GPS Data

    View and remove camera info, date/time and GPS location from photos.

  • Watermark Images

    Stamp text or a logo onto images in bulk to protect product photos.

  • Add Text to Image

    Write text on images with your choice of font, color, outline and shadow.

  • Merge Images

    Join multiple images horizontally, vertically or in a grid.

  • Split Image into Grid

    Cut one image into 2, 3, 6 or 9 tiles (for Instagram, Facebook).

  • Favicon Generator

    Create a .ico favicon and a full icon set for web, Apple and Android from one image.

  • SVG to PNG

    Convert SVG vector images to PNG/JPG at any resolution.

  • Image to Base64

    Convert images to Base64 strings / data URIs to embed in HTML and CSS.

  • Image Color Picker

    Tap an image to get color codes and extract its dominant palette automatically.

  • GIF Maker

    Turn several images into an animated GIF, adjusting speed and size.

  • Image to Text (OCR)

    Recognize text in photos and scans, including accented Vietnamese.

  • Signature & Stamp Extractor

    Extract a signature or stamp photographed on paper into a transparent PNG.

  • Telegram Sticker Downloader

    Download whole Telegram sticker sets: original files (WEBP, TGS, WEBM) or converted to PNG and GIF; also converts .tgs and .webm files you already have.
