Audio & Video tools

Voice and screen recording, trim and merge audio, video to MP3, compress video. 11 free tools that run right in your browser.

  • Voice Recorder

    Record with your microphone, pause, play back, name and download as M4A, MP3 or WAV.

  • Screen Recorder

    Record your screen, a window or a tab with system audio and microphone (on computers), download as MP4/WebM.

  • Trim Audio

    Cut audio clips and make ringtones (including iPhone M4R), with fade in/out effects.

  • Merge Audio

    Join several audio files into one: back to back, with silence gaps or crossfades, with volume normalization.

  • Audio Converter

    Convert between MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG; choose quality and mono/stereo, many files at once.

  • Video to MP3

    Extract the audio from a video into an MP3 (or M4A, WAV), the whole video or a segment.

  • Video to GIF

    Cut a video segment and turn it into an animated GIF, choosing size, frame rate and speed.

  • Trim Video

    Cut out the part of a video you need, keeping the original quality (no re-encoding) or frame-accurate.

  • Compress Video

    Reduce video size for messaging and email, by quality or target size, using your device's hardware encoder.

  • Video Frame Capture

    Grab full-resolution images from any frame of a video, or capture automatically every N seconds.

  • Text to Speech

    Read text aloud with the voices built into your device, highlighting the current sentence, with adjustable speed.
