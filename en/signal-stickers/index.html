Signal Sticker Downloader

Paste a signal.art/addstickers link to download and decrypt a whole Signal sticker pack right in your browser, as WEBP, PNG or GIF.

BbeTools has no server: your browser downloads the encrypted pack from Signal's server (cdn-ca.signal.org) and decrypts it itself. The pack_key is only used on your device and never sent anywhere.

How to use

  1. In Signal, open the sticker pack → Share → Copy link (like signal.art/addstickers/#pack_id=…&pack_key=…).
  2. Paste it into the Link box and click “Get sticker pack”; the page downloads and decrypts the whole pack.
  3. Choose PNG + GIF or the original format (WEBP, APNG), then click “Download all (.zip)” or click individual stickers.

Frequently asked questions

Why do Signal stickers need decrypting?

Signal encrypts every sticker pack on its server (AES-256); only people with the full link have the key (pack_key) to open it. The page uses the key in the link to decrypt right in your browser with Web Crypto, so BbeTools never sees your stickers or key.

My link says the key is wrong?

The link must include both pack_id (32 characters) and pack_key (64 characters). If you only copied part of it, open the pack in Signal again → Share → Copy link.

What format are Signal stickers?

Still stickers are usually WEBP; animated ones are animated PNG (APNG) or animated WEBP. Choose “PNG + GIF” for files that open everywhere; choose “Original format” to keep full quality for reuse in Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp.

Can I use downloaded stickers commercially?

Stickers are copyrighted by their creators. Download them for personal use only, or with the creator's permission.
