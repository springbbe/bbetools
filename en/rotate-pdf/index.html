Rotate PDF

Rotate individual pages or the whole PDF to the right reading orientation.

Your files are processed right on this device and never uploaded to any server.

Accepts: PDF

How to use

  1. Choose or drag and drop a PDF file.
  2. Click an upside-down page to rotate it 90° (↺ ↻ to rotate left/right), or rotate all pages at once.
  3. Click “Rotate PDF” and download the rotated file.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every step runs in JavaScript right in your browser; files never leave your device.

Does rotating blur text or images?

No. The tool only changes the page's display orientation in the file; content keeps its quality.

Can I rotate just a few pages?

Yes. Click exactly the pages to rotate; the others stay as they are.
