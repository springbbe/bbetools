BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index with Asian and WHO international classifications.

The numbers you enter are only calculated in your browser and never sent anywhere.

How to use

  1. Enter your height (cm) and weight (kg).
  2. Choose the Asian classification (default) or the international WHO standard.
  3. See your BMI, its category and the healthy weight range for your height.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal BMI?

WHO: 18.5 to under 25 is normal, 25–30 overweight, 30+ obese. For Asians: 18.5 to under 23 is normal, 23 to under 25 overweight, 25+ obese.

Why do Asians use different thresholds?

At the same BMI, Asians usually have more body fat and a higher, earlier risk of heart disease and diabetes, so the WHO recommends lower thresholds for the Asia-Pacific region.
